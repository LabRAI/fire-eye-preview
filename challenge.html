How the challenge works

Participants run inference on their own computer or a service they control, including their own Hugging Face Space. Fire-eye accepts forecast data, not executable models, weights or containers. This challenge does not use laboratory compute.

Files, receipts, round definitions, observed evidence and results are versioned in one Hugging Face dataset. A GitHub-hosted job checks submissions and observations daily. Your deadline is shown in UTC; both the gateway receipt and HF publication must precede the cutoff.

The first track measures documented wildfire-discovery cell capture within the top 10% and 20% of ranked land area. Prescribed fires are excluded and tied scores receive proportional credit. Unreported cells are not assumed fire-free, so this track does not publish overall accuracy or PR-AUC. Windows without recorded fires receive no score.

Observations have a 72-hour reporting allowance. Results are preliminary until seven days after each window. Failed checks retain the last published result. FireWx-FM is compared only where a matching forecast was available before the cutoff; its 48-hour horizon is not extended to seven days.

GitHub verifies sign-in. Supabase keeps pseudonymous sessions and limited activity only. Forecast data and model metadata are stored in HF. Open the forecast archive ↗
