Prediction format & timing

Fill the template with your model's 0–1 scores in row-major order. All reference land cells require a value; ocean / NoData cells may remain null. Grid CSV supplies cell-centre coordinates and the reference land mask. Both models are compared at 0.001 precision.

The public receipt must be confirmed before the entire 12-hour window starts. Allow at least 20 minutes for collection and publication. A filename or self-reported issue date is not proof of an early prediction. Late submissions cannot enter the prospective comparison.

How comparison works

After a 72-hour reporting allowance, we compare how many documented wildfire-discovery cells fall inside each model's highest-ranked 10% and 20% of the same land area. Ties receive proportional weight. Results are refreshed through day seven.

This first track uses NIFC/WFIGS wildfire reports and excludes prescribed-fire records. Discovery reports are not a full satellite-occupancy target. Unreported cells are not verified fire-free cells; no overall accuracy, false-alarm rate or probability calibration is inferred. Participants may submit after the reference issue and use later inputs; this is a paired case comparison, not a controlled equal-lead benchmark. Different submitted regions are not pooled into a leaderboard.

Submissions & comparisons

The collector runs independently every ten minutes. Forecast values and receipts are retained when observations or publication are delayed. The live map does not wait for this evaluation.
